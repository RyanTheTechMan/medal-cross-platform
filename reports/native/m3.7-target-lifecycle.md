# M3.7 target lifecycle cleanup checkpoint

Date: 2026-09-19
Checkpoint: `7dfaa6f`
Scope: prevent a failed ScreenCaptureKit application stream from overlapping the next automatic target.

## Implemented behavior

When ScreenCaptureKit reports `source_disappeared`/`failed`, the helper now gates automatic discovery on the capture session state. A failed stream is explicitly driven through `stop()` and must reach `stopped` before the next PID-aware application target is selected. `starting`, `capturing`, `stopping`, and picker states are not eligible for a replacement target. This keeps the old delegate stream from racing a newly selected process.

The change is native-only; no raw frames, encoded packets, or process-window payloads cross Electron IPC.
